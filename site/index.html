Why myTeams

Every team you follow, in one place

Built with SwiftUI against ESPN's public site API. No account, no API keys, no tracking.

  • ◎

    All leagues in one app

    Pick teams from NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, MLS, WNBA, NCAA Football, NCAA Men's and Women's Basketball and the Premier League. Search falls back to ESPN's own, so teams elsewhere can be followed too.

  • ●

    Live scores + alerts

    One scoreboard poll per league covers every favorite in it. Get alerts for the start, each score, each period's end and the final while the app is open.

  • ▣

    Widgets + Live Activities

    A "Team Schedule" widget for the home screen and Lock Screen shows your team's next game. Games in progress get a Live Activity with score, period and clock on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

  • ⚽

    European soccer coverage

    Premier League, LALIGA and more via ESPN's public API — no account, no API keys.

  • ◇

    Private by design

    No account needed and no tracking — the app collects no data. It works straight from ESPN's public site.

  • ☁

    Favorites that sync

    Your followed teams are mirrored to iCloud key-value storage, so they sync between your devices.

Features

What the app does

A page for every team

One page per followed team, with everything in one scroll:

  • A roster carousel with filter and sort menus, and a player sheet with season stats.
  • The team's stat leaders and the league's leaderboards.
  • The team's news feed.
Roster
Filter, sort, tap a player for season stats.
Schedule
Opens at the next game.
Standings
League table with your team picked out.
Leaders & news
Team and league leaders, plus the news feed.

Your favorites in the tab bar

The system tab bar has a tab per followed team, with its crest and short name; those past the bar's room sit under "More". The "Teams" tab opens the picker, where teams are followed, reordered and removed. The bar minimizes as you scroll, like the system's own apps.

Team crests
One tab per team, in your order.
Teams tab
Follow, reorder and remove teams.
Onboarding
A fresh install opens on "Pick Your Teams".

Schedule cards, box scores and standings

The schedule carousel opens at the next game. Tap a game for its box score, which refreshes while the game is live. The league standings show the team's row picked out, so you can see where it sits at a glance.

Your team's row highlighted in the league table.
